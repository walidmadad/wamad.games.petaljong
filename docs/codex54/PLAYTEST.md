# Petaljong CODEX 54 human playtest (not yet performed)

Use a test installation/profile or isolated fixture; never overwrite the player's saved puzzle.
Compare v11 and v12 with the same testers and tier order, counterbalancing old/new order.
Use Hard 8/12/15, Very Hard 16/23/30, early Expert 31/40/50, advanced Expert 51/75/100, and 150/201/501 for progression.
Record generator version, level, completion, retries, Hint/Undo use and elapsed time. No human results have been collected.

- Before each collection, ask the tester to name their intended partner/unlock when practical. Does planning change their choice?
- Count obvious opening pairs and sequences completed entirely by taking immediately available pairs.
- Record meaningful decisions with two or three distinct symbols held. Could the tester explain what a fourth symbol would do?
- At gameplay size, identify peony/camellia, marigold/chrysanthemum, crocus/bellflower, monstera/eucalyptus and both new maples. Note misidentifications separately from strategic errors.
- Inspect a mystery, switch to another, return using memory, and use the normal-board direct match. Check that the two-tap collection and single active reveal are clear.
- After a loss, can the tester identify the decision that filled the tray and describe a better alternative? Flag losses caused by information that could not reasonably be discovered.
- Ask for difficulty, fairness and enjoyment scores from 1–5, plus one concrete frustrating or satisfying decision.
- Confirm comfortable touch targets, no clipping, legible blocked symbols, matching tray artwork, and unchanged tutorial/accessibility.

Acceptance needs observed planning and understandable losses, not merely longer completion times. Automated solver results do not substitute for this playtest.
